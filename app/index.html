Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

Built from the official descriptions and PowerPyx’s roadmap (difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough, no mission replay, cheats disable trophies). The story takes 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist about 100 more; cleanup about 10.

Stage 1 · Play the story

Chapters 1–5 · 60–80 hours

Finish all five chapters and the named story missions. Every chapter and mission trophy pops automatically — none of them can be missed. On the way, try each activity type once, scan vehicles and study animals, and do the Chapter 1 meditation (see Missables).

Stage 2 · The 100% checklist

After the story · about 100 hours

After the credits the checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion: 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, any 70 of 80 challenges, 100 optional missions, 300 stunts, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations. Nothing here is missable.

Stage 3 · Strangers & cleanup

Free-roam · about 10 hours

Finish the character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, the money and property trophies, and the five guides still marked unconfirmed at launch. Then the platinum pops.

Missables — read this first

Only one trophy is potentially missable: Positive Mental Attitude. Its first meditation, at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, can only be done in Chapter 1 — after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept. It is still unconfirmed whether that one counts toward the trophy, but do it to be safe. (PowerPyx)

There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — the game offers 15 manual save slots plus one autosave (PowerPyx). Optional missions and some quest givers disappear for a while, but every trophy-related character comes back later, so nothing else locks you out of the platinum. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off for a trophy run.
